Lesson structure - EN

Lesson 1: Welcome & Overview
Title: "Welcome to Qalbsalim: Your Journey to Sustainable Success"

Description:
Welcome to Qalbsalim! 

In this first lesson, you'll get to know our app that helps you grow toward success in this life and the next. We'll show you how Qalbsalim is structured, what it can mean for you, and how you can use the main features. Discover how to work with Qalbsalim toward a life that encompasses both worldly and spiritual goals.

Objectives:
1.	Understand what Qalbsalim is and the main principles behind the app
2.	Get familiar with the general structure and main features
3.	Know which sections and tools are available and their purposes
4.	Understand how different parts work together 
5.	See how the 6-lesson onboarding can help you make optimal use of the app

Lesson 2: Islam as a Life System - Heart, Actions & Success
Title: "Islam as a Life System: Heart, Actions & Success"

Description:
In this deeper lesson, we discover the central philosophical framework of Qalbsalim: the Wheel of Islam. We explore how Falah (comprehensive success) forms the core, surrounded by Fiqh (practical implementation in actions) and Tazkiyyah (heart purification). You learn how these three elements work together for a complete Islamic life and how to access each aspect in the app for your personal growth.

Objectives:
1.	Understand the three-in-one model of Islamic life
2.	See how Fiqh and Tazkiyyah reinforce each other in practice
3.	Know where to find spiritual and practical content in the app
4.	Understand how personal growth works both internally and externally
5.	Learn how to use the Wheel of Islam as a guide for your development

Lesson 3: Your Life Areas - Balance & Priorities
Title: "Your Life Areas: Balance between Personal, Professional & Business"

Description:
Which life areas are important to you? In this lesson, we help you identify and prioritize your life areas. We explore the three goal wheels - Personal Life, Work, and Business - and how to tailor them to your unique situation, values, and responsibilities. Discover how to develop a balanced approach that suits you.

Objectives:
1.	Identify personal life areas that need attention
2.	Understand balance between different life roles and priorities
3.	Know how to align goals with personal values and beliefs
4.	Learn how to manage and monitor different domains in the app
5.	Develop a personal approach to life balance

Lesson 4: From Dream to Action - Your Goals

Title: "From Dream to Action: Bringing Your Goals to Life"

Description:
Successful people turn dreams into concrete steps. In this practical lesson, you learn how to set goals that are both challenging and achievable within your Islamic life vision. We use the OKR framework (Objectives and Key Results) to help you turn big dreams into concrete actions. Discover how to break down goals into measurable results and initiatives you can start immediately.

Objectives:
1.	Learn to set effective goals using the OKR model within Islamic frameworks
2.	Understand the difference between goals and measurable results
3.	Know how to break down goals into concrete actions and initiatives
4.	Learn how to visualize and track your progress in the app
5.	Develop a system for continuous improvement and growth

Lesson 5: Tazkiyyah - Strengthen Your Heart & Mind

Title: "Tazkiyyah: Purification and Strengthening of Your Heart"

Description:
A sound heart is the foundation of a successful life. Tazkiyyah is about purifying and strengthening your inner self. In this lesson, you discover how to integrate spiritual practices into your daily life. We offer practical exercises, reflection questions, and reading materials to help you grow in patience, gratitude, humility, and other important virtues essential for your spiritual growth.

Objectives:
1.	Understand Tazkiyyah as a practical spiritual discipline for daily life
2.	Learn how to develop daily spiritual habits and routines
3.	Know which exercises and practices are suitable for your personal situation
4.	Understand how to measure and track your spiritual growth in the app
5.	Develop a personal plan for heart purification and strengthening

Lesson 6: Fiqh - Practical Islam in Your Daily Life

Title: "Fiqh: Practical Islam in Your Daily Life"

Description:
Islam is not just faith, but also practice. Fiqh helps you apply Islamic principles in your daily decisions. In this lesson, you learn how to use practical Islamic knowledge in your work, relationships, finances, and other life areas. We provide concrete examples, scenarios, and exercises to help you live Islam rather than just understand it.

Objectives:
1.	Understand Fiqh as the practical application of Islam in daily situations
2.	Learn how to apply Islamic principles in work, relationships, and finances
3.	Know where to find practical Islamic advice and guidance
4.	Understand how to integrate Fiqh knowledge into your daily decision-making
5.	Develop skills for practical application of Islamic principles
